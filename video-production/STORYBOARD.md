### Scene Details

#### Scene 1: Cinematic Pune Opening (0.0s – 6.0s / 180 frames)
- **Visuals**: Slow cinematic aerial tracking shot across the Sahyadri ridges and historic Sinhagad ramparts with warm sunrise golden hour lighting.
- **Typography Overlay**:
  - Main: "PUNE, MAHARASHTRA" (Tracking: +4px, SemiBold)
  - Sub: "A Land of Historic Forts, Sacred Sanctuaries & Living Heritage"
- **Camera**: Slow optical push-in with gentle atmospheric fog and depth of field.
- **Sound Design**: Low frequency cinematic bass swell, subtle mountain breeze, ambient tanpura resonance.

#### Scene 2: Brand Reveal & Homepage Introduction (6.0s – 14.0s / 240 frames)
- **Visuals**: Transition to a sleek dark-slate studio environment featuring a 4K desktop display mockup with soft directional key lighting and ambient floor reflections. The real Explore Pune homepage is active.
- **Motion**: Camera glides from an oblique angle smoothly centering on the hero section.
- **Typography Overlay**: "EXPLORE PUNE" • "The Modern Digital Guide to 106+ Historic Landmarks"
- **Interactions**: Smooth scroll down revealing curated highlights and zero-friction navigation.
- **Sound Design**: Gentle whoosh transition into warm melodic synth and rhythmic strings.

#### Scene 3: Destination Discovery (14.0s – 25.0s / 330 frames)
- **Visuals**: Seamless transition into the destination discovery engine (`/destinations`).
- **Interactions**:
  - Filter pill clicks: "Forts", "Temples", "Heritage", "Nature".
  - Instant live search: typing "Sinhagad" with instant auto-suggest.
  - Hover highlight on verified destination cards with entry fees, visiting hours, and high-resolution imagery.
- **Typography Overlay**: "DISCOVER 106 VERIFIED DESTINATIONS" • "Real-Time Hours • Authentic Photos • Ticket Fees"
- **Sound Design**: Delicate glass UI clicks and melodic pulse.

- **Typography Overlay**: "INTERACTIVE 3D GIS MAP" • "Vector Category Pins • Exact Coordinates • Live Elevation"

- **Typography Overlay**: "SMART TRIP PLANNING" • "Tailored Itineraries • Transit Optimization • Export to PDF"
